A Hill Country Getaway Beyond Helotes

Helotes sits where San Antonio's suburbs meet the Hill Country, and it is where Bandera Road (Highway 16) starts heading out into ranch country. Whether you are visiting family in Helotes, coming for a show at Floore's Country Store, or simply want to get out of the city, there are hotels around Helotes, but a lot of guests want more than a room.

Al's Hideaway is a family-run property of 13 handcrafted log cabins, 10 full-hookup RV sites and 9 cowboy campsites on 20 acres in Pipe Creek. Follow Highway 16 north from Helotes and you are there in about 20 minutes. We are not a hotel; we are the quiet weekend that starts after the last stoplight.

Things to Do Near Helotes and on the Way Up

Helotes is known for live music at Floore's Country Store and for its access to Government Canyon State Natural Area, just to the west. Head north on Highway 16 and the Hill Country opens up: scenic drives, horseback riding and longhorns, river tubing and Bandera's rodeos and Main Street.

Once you are at Al's Hideaway, our neighbors add Highland cows, a camel farm and exotic animal encounters. The Hill Country area guide has the full list.

Hot Meals, Specialty Coffee & Clean Energy Drinks On Site

If you are escaping the traffic around Helotes and San Antonio, the whole point is to slow down. Our on-site store serves hot meals, and the Al's Hideaway Energy & Drink Bar is now open, making specialty coffee, espresso drinks and cold brew, plus clean energy drinks made with premium Monin flavors and sugar-free options. You can eat, sip and stock up without getting back in the car.

Why drive for morning coffee and breakfast when it is already here? Take a short walk to the store and listen to the birds along the way.

Then unplug. After a day of exploring there is no traffic to sit in, no car horns and no sirens — just the creeks, the pool, the quiet, and a sky full of stars. It is a place to actually relax and recharge.
