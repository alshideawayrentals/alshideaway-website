A Fall Getaway in the Texas Hill Country: Cabins Near Bandera and Boerne

By Gordon Cook · October 5, 2026

By Gordon Cook · Published October 5, 2026 · ← All posts

The short answer: Late October and November are one of the best times for a Hill Country getaway. The days are mild, the nights are cool and quiet, and there is always something going on in Bandera and Boerne. Al's Hideaway in Pipe Creek has 13 handcrafted log cabins, 10 full-hookup RV sites, and 9 cowboy campsites, about 15 minutes from both towns. Pets stay with no pet fee, there are no cleaning fees, and promo code FUN takes 10% off two nights or more when you book direct.

Howdy, folks. The heat has finally let up, and this is the part of the year Jennifer and I like best. If you have been thinking about getting away for a few days, here is how I would spend them.

Why Fall Is the Best Time for a Hill Country Getaway

Summer in Texas is hot, and by October most folks are ready to be outside again. You can sit on a covered porch with your coffee in the morning and still need a jacket at night. The roads are good for a scenic drive, the towns have events on the calendar, and the crowds are smaller than they will be at Christmas.

A Simple Getaway Weekend

Day one: slow down. Check in, drop your bags in your cabin, and do nothing for a while. Cook a good dinner in the outdoor kitchen or the cabin. After dark, step outside. We are far from city lights, and the stars out here are the real thing.

Day two: see the area. Drive 15 minutes to Bandera, the Cowboy Capital of the World, for lunch and some shopping. Or head the other way to downtown Boerne. Our scenic drives page has routes if you would rather just ride and look.

Day three: take it easy again. Walk the property, visit with the other guests, and let your dog enjoy the place too. Leave when you are good and rested, not when the checkout clock says so.

What Is Happening in the Second Half of October and November

  • Through November 2: Dia de los Muertos Tradition Trail, San Antonio. Altars and art along the River Walk. We have an altar on the trail honoring Jennifer's grandfather, Albert Banis.
  • October 23–25: Cactus Rose Endurance Trail Race at Hill Country State Natural Area near Bandera. Great weekend to cheer on a runner and rest up after.
  • November 7: Bandera Market Days at the Bandera County Courthouse, 9 AM, put on by the Bandera Business Association.
  • Thanksgiving, November 26: Boerne's Dickens on Main is on Thanksgiving weekend, and the Old West Christmas Light Fest opens November 27. Boerne has not posted exact Dickens dates yet.

Go Midweek and Skip the Crowds

If your schedule allows, come Sunday through Thursday. Roads are quieter, restaurants have open tables, and the property is calm. Midweek is also a smart way to see the Hill Country's popular state parks, which get busy on weekends.

Why Stay at Al's Hideaway

We are the closest campground to the new Kronkosky State Natural Area, and we are a short drive from Bandera, Boerne, and San Antonio. Our log cabins are handcrafted, our RV sites are pull-through, and our cowboy campsites are there for tent campers. Dogs are welcome with no pet fee, and we do not charge cleaning fees. Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time for a Texas Hill Country getaway?

Fall is hard to beat. Days are mild, nights are cool, and the summer heat is gone. Late October through November has fairs, markets, trail races, and Thanksgiving events, and midweek stays are quieter than weekends.

Are pets allowed at Al's Hideaway, and is there a pet fee?

Yes, pets are welcome, and there is no pet fee. There are also no cleaning fees or resort fees. Book direct and use promo code FUN for 10% off stays of two nights or more.

How far is Al's Hideaway from Bandera, Boerne, and San Antonio?

Al's Hideaway is at 299 Willow Springs Drive in Pipe Creek, Texas. It is about 15 minutes from Bandera, about 15 minutes from Boerne, and about 45 minutes from San Antonio. It is also the closest campground to the new Kronkosky State Natural Area.
