Flexible Ways to Pay for Your Venue

We offer several ways to pay, so the venue cost can work with your budget.

💳

Pay for Everything Yourself

One payment covers the whole park — your guests stay in the cabins, RV sites, and tent sites at no cost to them. The simplest option for hosts who want to treat their guests.

🤝

Split It with Your Guests

You set the price you want to charge your guests for cabins and sites. Their payments go toward the rental, and you simply pay the remaining balance. A popular way to make a full park rental surprisingly affordable.

Prefer a Smaller, Intimate Wedding?

Not every wedding needs the whole park. If you're planning a smaller, more intimate ceremony, you can simply reserve the cabins, RV sites, or campsites your group needs — and celebrate in one of the most beautiful settings in the Hill Country.

Please note that reserving individual accommodations doesn't guarantee exclusive use of all the amenities — other guests may be enjoying the park too. For full privacy and exclusive use of the entire property, choose the 12-hour or 2-day park rental.

Not sure which option fits? Call us at 830-510-3331 — we'll talk through your guest count and plans and help you find the right fit.

Wedding & Event Venue FAQ

The questions couples and event planners ask us most.

How much does it cost to rent Al's Hideaway for a wedding or event?

The full 2-day exclusive park rental is $13,619.98 and the 12-hour park rental is $7,473.89 — both prices include all taxes. Overnight stays are an add-on with the 12-hour option. No hidden fees, ever.

What's included with the venue rental?

Exclusive use of the entire park — all 13 cabins, 10 RV sites, and tent campsites — plus the event pavilion with outdoor kitchen, stone fireplace and big-screen TV, the swimming pool, and 8 round tables with 6 chairs each (seating for 48). You bring decorations, any extra tables and chairs, and all food and drinks.

Can we bring our own caterer, food, and drinks?

Yes! You supply your own food and drinks, and outside caterers are welcome. No required vendor lists, no corkage fees — your menu, your way.

Do you have an officiant for wedding ceremonies?

Yes — Gordon, co-owner of Al's Hideaway, is an ordained minister and can perform your wedding ceremony right on the property. Ask about officiant services when you book.

Do we have to rent the whole park for a small wedding?

No. For a smaller, intimate wedding you can reserve just the cabins, RV sites, or campsites your group needs. Keep in mind that individual reservations don't guarantee exclusive use of all the amenities — for full privacy, choose a park rental.

How can we pay for the venue?

We offer several ways to pay. You can pay for everything yourself, or set the price you want to charge your guests for cabins and sites — their payments go toward the rental and you pay the remaining balance.
