Rides & Rallies Near Al's Hideaway

Seven Reasons Riders & Drivers Keep Coming Back

We're tucked right in the middle of the routes you came to ride — close enough to be back in time for a campfire, far enough to feel like you got away.

Three Twisted Sisters

⭐ Most Famous 🏍️ Biker Favorite

The signature ride of the Texas Hill Country: Ranch Roads 335, 336 & 337 form a roughly 100-mile loop through Medina, Vanderpool, and Leakey — just 10 minutes from Al's Hideaway. RR 337 alone packs more than 65 curves into 15 miles.

  • Riders refuel at the Lone Star Motorcycle Museum in Vanderpool
  • Or the Frio Canyon Motorcycle Stop in Leakey
  • Best on weekday mornings — weekends bring heavy bike traffic
📍 10 min from Al's Hideaway

Willow City Loop

🌸 Wildflower Route

A scenic 13-mile loop near Fredericksburg, popular with riders and photographers alike, especially during bluebonnet season (late March–April).

  • Passes entirely through private property — stay on the road
  • Weekday mornings see the least traffic
📍 ~45 min from Al's Hideaway

Hwy 16 — "The Sweet 16"

🛣️ Connector Route

A favorite connector route between Bandera and the Sisters, with rolling hills and river crossings — easy to combine into a full-day loop.

  • Direct link between Bandera and Medina/Kerrville
📍 Starts in Bandera

Texas Hill Country BDR-X

🧭 Overland Route

The official Backcountry Discovery Route through Central Texas — roughly 650 miles of dirt roads, low-water crossings, bump gates, and sections of the Twisted Sisters. Rideable July through September.

  • Starts near Fredericksburg; Day 1 runs through Bandera
  • Free GPS tracks available from ridebdr.com
📍 Bandera leg nearby

Thunder in the Hill Country

🎪 Spring Rally

Spring motorcycle rally at Mansfield Park in Bandera with live music, bike shows, and on-site camping.

  • March 26–29, 2026
  • Weekend passes start at $60
📍 Mansfield Park, Bandera

Rumble on the River

🎪 Fall Rally

Fall bike rally at Mansfield Park with poker runs, a custom bike show, and live music. Adult-only weekend event.

  • September 25–27, 2026
📍 Mansfield Park, Bandera

Porsche & Corvette Club Touring

🏎️ Car Clubs

Sports car clubs run the same twisty roads on weekday drives. The Hill Country Rallye each March draws vintage air-cooled Porsches through Kerrville, and local PCA chapters tour the Sisters and Hwy 16 regularly.

  • Hill Country Region PCA — year-round local touring
  • Hill Country Rallye — every March, Kerrville
📍 Kerrville & surrounding loops

Built by Our Own Team

Scenic Stop Map: Kerrville, Hunt & Lost Maples Loop

A roughly 190-mile loop starting and ending at Al's Hideaway — a full day of Hill Country backroads with real stops along the way.

  1. StartAl's Hideaway — 299 Willow Springs Dr, Pipe Creek, TX. Ride start/finish.
  2. 37.2 miTX-16 N and TX-173 S into Kerrville, TX — stop at the Wall of Hope on Water St.
  3. 12.5 miTX-27 W to TX-39 W toward Hunt, TX — stop at The Hunt Store, 1634 TX-39, Hunt, TX.
  4. 32.5 miTX-39 and US-83 N to Garven Store, 27304 US-83, Mountain Home, TX.
  5. 47.1 miTX-39 E and RM 187 to Lost Maples Cafe, 384 Main St, Utopia, TX.
  6. ~miFM 470 toward Bandera, TX — pass through downtown, including the Bandera General Store.
  7. 47.2 miTX-16 S back to Al's Hideaway.
