What’s Offered

New to any of these? Here’s a plain-English look at each one. Every service starts with a consultation with Dr. Warren.

💊

Hormone Pellets

What it is: Bioidentical hormone replacement in a tiny pellet about the size of a grain of rice. After the area is numbed, the pellet is placed just under the skin, usually in the upper hip, in a quick in-office procedure. It releases a steady, low dose of hormone over the next few months, so there are no daily creams or pills to remember.

Often used for: Women and men with symptoms of low hormones, such as hot flashes, low energy, poor sleep or low sex drive. Lab work is usually needed first so Dr. Warren can find the right dose.

💧

IV Therapy

What it is: Fluids, vitamins and electrolytes given through a small IV in the arm, so they go straight into your bloodstream instead of through your stomach. Most drips take about 30 to 60 minutes, so you can sit back and relax while it runs.

Often used for: Rehydrating and recharging after travel, a hard week or a busy season, or getting a vitamin boost.

🧬

Peptide Therapy

What it is: Peptides are short chains of amino acids, the building blocks of protein, that your body uses as signals. Peptide therapy uses specific peptides, usually given as small injections just under the skin, picked to match your goals.

Often used for: People working on goals like recovery, sleep, energy or body composition. Dr. Warren will go over which options fit your goals and health history.

⚖️

Weight Loss Shots

What it is: Prescription weekly injections in the GLP-1 family of medications, such as semaglutide or tirzepatide. They work with your body's natural appetite signals to help you feel full sooner and stay full longer, and they're used along with healthy eating and activity.

Often used for: Adults who want medical support with weight loss. A consultation and health review comes first to make sure the shots are a safe fit for you.
