Discover Al's Hideaway: A Family-Built Hill Country Retreat with Heart

By Gordon Cook · January 22, 2026

By Gordon Cook · January 22, 2026 · ← All posts

When people search for cabins, RV sites, or campgrounds in Bandera County, they're often looking for more than a place to sleep. They want peace, connection, comfort, and a genuine Hill Country experience. That's exactly what inspired us to create Al's Hideaway — a family-built retreat in Pipe Creek, Texas, designed with intention, handcrafted details, and a whole lot of heart.

Who We Are: A Family with a Vision and a Legacy

Al's Hideaway isn't a corporate campground or a mass-produced resort. It's a dream built from the ground up by the Cook family — with our own hands, our own sweat, and our own love for the Hill Country.

Our name honors Al, a beloved grandfather and WWII veteran whose strength, resilience, and spirit inspired us to create a place where people could rest, reconnect, and feel at home. His legacy lives in every cabin beam, every trail, and every guest we welcome.

We live on-site, maintain the property ourselves, and greet guests personally. When you stay here, you're not just booking a site — you're joining a community that values kindness, connection, and authenticity.

What We Offer

Handcrafted Log Cabins

Our 13 cabins aren't prefab units. They're true log cabins, built with craftsmanship and designed for comfort: private bathrooms, climate control, cozy living spaces, and rustic charm with modern amenities. Several include full kitchens. Perfect for couples, families, or anyone who wants the comfort of home in a natural setting.

Spacious RV Sites

Our 10 pull-through RV sites offer full hookups, plenty of space, easy access, and peaceful surroundings — whether you're a weekend traveler or a long-term RVer.

Primitive Tent Camping

For those who want to unplug and reconnect with nature, our 9 cowboy campsites offer privacy, shade, and the sounds of the Hill Country all around you.

Community Spaces

We designed Al's Hideaway to bring people together: a large pavilion, fire pit, outdoor seating areas, a pool, and gathering spaces for events, reunions, and celebrations.

The Al's Hideaway Difference

Bandera County has many places to stay — but none quite like us. We didn't buy a campground; we built one. Guests often tell us they arrive as strangers and leave as friends. Our cabins feel like home, not rentals. You get the quiet of the Hill Country with easy access to Bandera, Boerne, Medina Lake, and San Antonio. And our reviews consistently highlight cleanliness, comfort, hospitality, and a peaceful atmosphere.

This isn't just a place to stay. It's a place to breathe. A place to reconnect. A place to feel at home.

If you're ready to experience the Texas Hill Country in a way that's authentic, comfortable, and full of heart, we'd love to welcome you.
