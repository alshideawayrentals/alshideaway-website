The Perfect Texas Hill Country Venue

When you rent Al's Hideaway for your event, you get exclusive access to the entire property — no shared spaces, no strangers wandering through your wedding. Just your group, 20 private acres, and everything we've built here.

The event pavilion features an outdoor kitchen, a beautiful stone fireplace, and a big-screen TV. Surround it with the pool, the campfire area, the walking trails, and 13 cabins sleeping your guests — and you've got a setting that no hotel ballroom can touch.

Gordon and Jennifer are on-site the entire time. You're not dealing with a venue coordinator who shows up an hour before — you're dealing with the people who built this place and genuinely care that your event is perfect.
