FAQ & Policies

Quick answers to the most common questions about staying at Al's Hideaway.

The Basics at a Glance

The most important things to know before you book. Want every detail? Read our full property policies.

🕐

Check-In / Check-Out

Check-in: 3:00 PM
Check-out: 11:00 AM
Early check-in or late check-out is available for $25 per hour — please arrange in advance.

🐾

Pet Policy

Pets are always welcome — and always free. Well-behaved dogs are allowed in all cabins, RV sites, and campsites. Please keep them leashed in common areas.

🧹

No Cleaning Fees

We never charge cleaning fees. What you see at checkout is what you pay. Please leave the cabin in reasonable condition.

🎉

Best Price Discount

Book 2+ nights online and use promo code FUN at checkout to save 10%. Booking direct always gives you the best rate.

🚬

Smoking Policy

Smoking is not permitted inside any cabin or tent — a $250 cleaning fee applies. Smoking is allowed outdoors; please dispose of materials safely.

🍻

Alcohol Policy

Alcohol is permitted on the property for guests 21+. Please drink responsibly and be considerate of other guests, especially in common areas in the evening.

🔊

Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are 11:00 PM – 7:00 AM. We want everyone to enjoy a peaceful stay — please be mindful of noise levels in the evenings.

🔥

Campfires

Campfires are allowed in designated fire rings only, provided we are not under a burn ban and it is not windy. Never leave a fire unattended.
