Where to Stay in and Around Boerne

Boerne (pronounced “Bernie”) has always been one of the prettiest small towns in the Hill Country, and now the rest of the country is noticing. It is the only Texas finalist in Hallmark Channel’s 2026 Merriest Christmas Town contest, and it is the setting for Bravo’s upcoming series Secrets, Lies, Texas Wives. That means more visitors, busier weekends and in-town rooms that book up fast, especially from Thanksgiving through December.

Al's Hideaway is the easy answer when town is full. We are a family-run Hill Country property in Pipe Creek, 15 minutes from Boerne straight down Highway 46 — just two red lights — with 13 log cabins, 10 full-hookup pull-through RV sites and 9 cowboy campsites on 20 acres. Spend the day on Main Street, then come back to a porch, two creeks and a sky full of stars.

Christmas in Boerne & Things to Do Year-Round

From Thanksgiving weekend through late December, Boerne celebrates with three themed holiday weekends. Dickens on Main is the big one: Victorian costumes, lights and holiday shopping along Main Street. With the Hallmark spotlight on the town this year, expect bigger crowds than usual. Book early, park once, and let the cabin be your quiet base. Our Christmas lights and parades guide has more holiday events around the Hill Country.

The rest of the year, spend the day on Main Street, walk the trails at the Cibolo Nature Center or head down into Cascade Caverns, then come back to the creeks and the stars.

Boerne is also a gateway to the wider Hill Country. Kronkosky State Natural Area is close by (see cabins near Kronkosky), and our area guide covers Bandera, Kerrville, Fredericksburg and San Antonio day trips.
