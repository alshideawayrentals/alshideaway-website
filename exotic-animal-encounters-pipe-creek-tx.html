A Private Zoo Experience, Just for Your Group

Happy Tails Entertainment is a fully licensed and insured wildlife education organization based in Pipe Creek, about 15 minutes from Bandera. Their private animal encounters take place at their home property and are closed to the public, so your family or small group has the animals to yourselves. There are no additional admission fees.

Expect hands-on time with capybaras, Bennett's wallabies, African crested porcupines, alpacas, Patagonian cavies, reptiles, and more, guided by friendly, knowledgeable handlers. You can ask questions, feed and pet many of the animals, and take plenty of photos.

Good to know: Encounters are by appointment only, for guests age 5 and up, in groups of up to 10. Reserve through the Happy Tails contact page, email admin@happytailsent.com, or call Anita at 210-385-4351 or Mark at 210-387-8460.
