Hotel, RV Park, or Cabin? Here's How to Pick

Bandera's Cowboy Capital reputation pulls in everyone from weekend couples to full families with a fifth-wheel in tow. What's "best" really depends on what you're after.

A Hotel in Town

Fine for a single night close to the honky-tonks on Main Street. But you're paying for a parking-lot view, a room built for sleeping, and no kitchen, fire pit, or space for the dog.

An RV Park

Great if you're already set up with a rig and just need a level, full-hookup pad. Worth checking whether it's just a gravel lot or an actual property with a pool, store, and shade.

A Real Log Cabin

Space to actually unwind — a kitchen, a porch, a fire pit, room for the whole family or the dog. The catch: a lot of Hill Country "cabin" listings are sheds or metal buildings dressed up for photos. Ours aren't.
