Bandera Lodging, Sorted Out

Bandera calls itself the Cowboy Capital of the World, and it earns it: rodeos, honky-tonks on Main Street, the Medina River and some of the best scenic drives in Texas. Most visitors start by searching for hotels or motels in Bandera — and for a single night close to the bars, a room in town does the job.

But if you are staying two or three nights, bringing the dog, towing a rig, or travelling with family, a room in town starts to feel small. Al's Hideaway is not a hotel. We are a family-run property of 13 handcrafted log cabins, 10 full-hookup RV sites and 9 cowboy campsites on 20 acres in Pipe Creek, 15 minutes from Bandera's Main Street. Below is how the options compare so you can pick what fits your trip.

What Staying 15 Minutes Out Really Means

Staying outside town is a trade. You will drive to Main Street (about 15 minutes), but you get the Hill Country the way it is meant to be: 20 acres, two creeks, wildlife, a pool, and one of the darkest sky corridors in Central Texas. Guests tell us the stars and the quiet are what they remember most.

From here you are close to everything Bandera is known for: the scenic drives, horseback riding and longhorns, river tubing, and our own neighbors' Highland cows, camel farm and exotic animal encounters. The Hill Country area guide has the full list.

Hot Meals, Specialty Coffee & Clean Energy Drinks On Site
