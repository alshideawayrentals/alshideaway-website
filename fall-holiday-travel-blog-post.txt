WHY IS FALL AND HOLIDAY TRAVEL MORE EXPENSIVE THAN SUMMER?
Summer in Texas is hot, and that keeps some travelers home. Fall is the opposite. Days are warm, nights are cool, and everyone wants to be outside. Then the holidays stack on top: Thanksgiving week, Christmas lights, parades, and holiday markets in Bandera, Boerne, Fredericksburg, and San Antonio all draw visitors at the same time.

When that many people want the same weekends, hotels and vacation rentals tend to raise nightly rates, set minimum stays, and add extra charges. The nightly rate is only part of the story. Before you book, add up the whole trip: the room, the fees, the parking, and every meal out.
