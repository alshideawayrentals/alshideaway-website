Your Peaceful Retreat During FIFA World Cup 2026

By Gordon Cook · November 14, 2025

By Gordon Cook · November 14, 2025 · ← All posts

The FIFA World Cup is coming to Texas in 2026, and the state will be buzzing with energy, fans, and unforgettable experiences. While the big cities host the matches, savvy travelers know the best way to enjoy the excitement without the chaos: escape to Al's Hideaway, a serene Hill Country retreat within easy reach of the action.

Why Choose Al's Hideaway During the World Cup?

  • Close to the action, far from crowds. Located in Pipe Creek, Al's Hideaway offers a peaceful getaway. After cheering for your favorite team, relax in nature instead of battling city traffic and inflated hotel prices.
  • Comfort meets adventure. Enjoy cozy cabins, RV sites, and tent camping surrounded by the beauty of the Texas Hill Country — perfect for families, groups, and solo travelers.
  • Explore more than soccer. Between matches, discover the San Antonio River Walk, wineries in Fredericksburg, and outdoor adventures in Bandera — the Cowboy Capital of the World.

What Makes Al's Hideaway Special?

  • Private cabins with modern amenities
  • Outdoor pool and fire pits for evening relaxation
  • Easy access to hiking, biking, and local dining
  • Family-owned hospitality that makes you feel at home

Book Early — World Cup Fans Are Coming!

With hundreds of thousands of visitors expected in Texas, accommodations will fill fast. Secure your spot at Al's Hideaway now and enjoy the best of both worlds: the thrill of the World Cup and the tranquility of the Hill Country.
