The Hill Country Weekend Just Outside San Antonio

San Antonio has the Alamo, the River Walk, world-class food and thousands of hotel rooms. What it does not have is quiet. Al's Hideaway is where San Antonians and visitors go when they want the Hill Country instead: 13 handcrafted log cabins, 10 full-hookup RV sites and 9 cowboy campsites on 20 acres in Pipe Creek, about 45 minutes from downtown.

We are not a hotel, and that is the point. There are no hallways or parking-lot views: two creeks, a pool, wildlife, and one of the darkest sky corridors in Central Texas. Jennifer and Gordon, the owners, are both certified San Antonio Hospitality Champions through Visit San Antonio, so we take being a great host to San Antonio's visitors seriously.

Hot Meals, Specialty Coffee & Clean Energy Drinks On Site

A big city is exciting and it is loud: traffic, car horns, sirens. Our on-site store serves hot meals, and the Al's Hideaway Energy & Drink Bar is now open, making specialty coffee, espresso drinks and cold brew, plus clean energy drinks made with premium Monin flavors and sugar-free options. You can eat, sip and stock up without getting back in the car.

Why drive for morning coffee and breakfast when it is already here? Take a short walk to the store and listen to the birds along the way.

Then unplug. After a day of exploring there is no traffic to sit in, no car horns and no sirens — just the creeks, the pool, the quiet, and a sky full of stars. It is a place to actually relax and recharge.
